Cookie policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

This site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It stores a small amount of information in your browser so that it works properly.

What is stored on your device

  • Sign-in: when you sign in, Google Firebase keeps you signed in using your browser's storage. With "Remember me" ticked you stay signed in on that browser until you sign out; without it you are signed out when you close the browser. Your choice is remembered on that browser. Staff who sign in without "Remember me" are also signed out after 30 minutes without activity in any tab, so the time of their last activity is kept in the browser too.
  • Offline copy: a copy of the pages' content, events and your tickets is cached so the site loads quickly and your tickets open with a weak connection.
  • Your bag: the tickets you have added but not yet booked.
  • Footer preference: whether you have folded the footer away.
  • Notices you have closed: that you pressed OK on the cookie notice, and, if you chose "Not now", that you do not want the offer to add BAI to your home screen. Each is a single yes, kept only on that browser.

These are needed for the site to do what you ask of it. None of them is used to track you across other websites, so there is nothing to opt in or out of: the short notice shown on your first visit is there to tell you this, and it does not change what is stored.

If you add BAI to your home screen, the app is this same website: it uses the same storage, and on an iPhone it keeps its own separate sign-in.

Other services

If you choose "Sign in with Google", Google opens its own window and sets its own cookies there, under Google's privacy policy. The site's typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts.

To keep automated abuse out, the site uses Google reCAPTCHA, which may set its own cookies and stores a short-lived proof in your browser that you are using the real site.

Clearing it

Signing out removes your sign-in. Clearing site data for this website in your browser settings removes everything listed above; your account and tickets stay safe on our side and return when you sign in again.
