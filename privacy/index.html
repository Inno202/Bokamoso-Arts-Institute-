Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information this website collects, why, and what you can do about it. The site is run by Bokamoso Arts Institute, 110 Industrial Park, Mabopane, Gauteng, 0190. We handle personal information in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address when you create an account. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store it. When you create an account with an email address, Firebase may send you a link to confirm it.
  • Tickets and orders: which events you booked, how many tickets, the price and its currency, the time of booking, how many tickets your account holds for each event, and when a ticket was scanned at the door and by which staff account.
  • Your bag: the tickets you have added but not yet booked, saved to your account and in your browser so they are still there when you come back.
  • Waiting lists and transfers: if you join an event's waiting list, your name, email address and the time you joined, which event staff can see so they can contact you. If you pass a ticket to a friend, the email address you enter is stored on the ticket until they accept, and your friend sees the email address of the account it came from.
  • At the door: so tickets can be checked where there is no signal, the phones of door staff keep a copy of the event's ticket list (ticket reference, type, status and the booking email address) until the event is over.
  • Event news: only if you tick "Email me about upcoming concerts and events" (when you create an account, or later in your account), we record that choice and when you made it, and may email you about upcoming events. The box is never ticked for you. Untick it in your account at any time and we stop. The CEO and super admins can see who asked for event news and copy those email addresses to send the news; it is sent with addresses hidden from other recipients.
  • Emails you send us: if you email us, we receive whatever you choose to send.
  • Staff activity: for staff accounts, your role, a record of which account last changed site content or scanned a ticket, a copy of each version of site content you save with your name and the time (kept so staff can look back and restore an earlier version; only staff who can edit site content see it), and an activity log of staff actions (such as role changes, event and content edits, and access approvals) with your name, role and the time. Only super admins can see the activity log, and its entries cannot be changed or deleted. If you ask for access to edit site content, we keep your name, email address, any note you add, when you asked, and who approved or declined it and when.
  • Error reports: when a page breaks, or a sign-in, booking or ticket scan fails for an unexpected reason, the site records the error message, the page, the type of browser (for example "Chrome on Android"), the site version and, if you are signed in, your account number. It never records your name, email address, password or what you typed. Only super admins can see these reports; we use them to fix problems.

We do not collect card or bank details on this site, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To issue your tickets, show them to you, and check them at the door.
  • To report internally on how many tickets were issued.
  • To reply when you contact us.

We do not sell your information, and we only email you about events if you asked for event news.

Who else handles it

  • Google Firebase stores accounts, tickets and site content, and handles sign-in. Its servers may be outside South Africa.
  • Cloudinary stores photographs that staff upload to the site.
  • Google reCAPTCHA (through Firebase App Check) checks that requests come from this website in a real browser and not from an automated script. It looks at device and browser signals to do this, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Outside ticket sellers: when you follow a link to buy tickets from another seller (such as a venue or a ticket site in another country), you leave this site. We do not pass them your details; whatever you give them is covered by their own privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website, and Google Fonts supplies its typefaces. Like any web host, they see your device's IP address when your browser loads the page.

How long we keep it

We keep your account until you delete it (on your Account page) or ask us to. Deleting it removes your profile, your bag and your sign-in. Ticket and order records are kept as a record of bookings and attendance. Ask us if you would like yours removed.

Your rights

You can change your name and password and delete your account yourself on your Account page. You can also ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email info@bokamosoarts.org. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Changes

If what we collect or how we use it changes, we will update this page and the date above.
