Terms of use

Last updated 16 November 2026

These terms apply when you use this website, create an account or book tickets. The site is run by Bokamoso Arts Institute, 110 Industrial Park, Mabopane, Gauteng, 0190.

Your account

You need an account to book tickets. Give your real name and an email address you can read, and keep your password to yourself. You are responsible for what is done through your account. We may deactivate an account that is misused.

Tickets

  • Online payment is not available yet. Tickets booked on this site are currently issued without charge, and the site never asks for card details. These terms will be updated before any payment is taken online.
  • Each event is priced in the currency shown next to its price, for example rand (R), pula (P) or emalangeni (E). Amounts in different currencies are shown separately and never converted or added together.
  • Tickets for some events, for example performances outside South Africa, are sold by another company such as the venue or a local ticket site. For those events the Events page links to that seller and no ticket is issued on this site. The seller's own prices, terms, refund rules and privacy policy apply, and any questions about those tickets go to them.
  • Each ticket admits one person, once. It is checked and marked as used at the door.
  • One account can hold up to 10 tickets for an event, while seats remain.
  • Your ticket reference and QR code are your proof of booking. Anyone who has them can use the ticket, so only share them with the person attending.
  • Event dates, times and venues can change. The Events page and your ticket always show the current details.

Using the site

Do not try to break, overload or gain unauthorised access to the site, book tickets with automated tools, or use another person's account.
